# Adoption and Consumer Boundary

## Current adopters

### repository-health-assessment 5.2

RHA is the reference extraction source and first adopter. It keeps its health island, eleven-surface denominator, criticality weights, lane scorecards, caps, and confidence as the only health-grade authority. `assets/evidence-recon-projection.js` emits a conservative expedition-mode sidecar for generic downstream use. Negative health observations are not promoted to supported absence without an explicit receipt.

### expanded-grill-with-docs 3.2

Expanded Grill creates one validated packet before its mandatory decision-coverage sweep. Bounded evidence stays inline; wide evidence may use expedition lanes. The packet removes discoverable factual questions and seeds possible decision surfaces, but cannot waive the sweep, settle human intent, or choose flat versus graph representation.

### decision-terrain-mapper 3.1

Decision Terrain Mapper uses the same packet before charting or ticket-local evidence work. Mapping scouts are expedition lanes only when evidence breadth warrants them. The packet can answer factual candidates but cannot prove terrain completeness, close fog, or mutate map lifecycle state.

## Candidate adopters

Repository Improvement Assessment, the integrity and architecture assessment skills, Connected Impact Sweep, and Adaptive Causal Debugging can adopt the primitive later. They are listed as candidates rather than current consumers until their own authority contracts, fixtures, and validators have been migrated.

## Integration law

A consumer may specialize the shared vocabulary but may not fork it. Domain-specific scoring, denominators, decision graphs, grades, and verification remain consumer-owned. Shared packet validation is a prerequisite for generic evidence handoff, not permission to bypass the consumer's own completeness or authority gates.
